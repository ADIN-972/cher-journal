## Known Limitations & Future Work

### Current Limitations (by design)
1. **Single Active Schema**: Only one schema can be active at a time
   - *Future:* Support multiple schemas with priority/region

2. **No Approval Workflow**: Prices change immediately
   - *Future:* Add CFO approval requirement

3. **No Scheduled Changes**: Price changes effective immediately
   - *Future:* Schedule changes for future dates

### Future Enhancements (Phase 2+)
1. **Bulk Operations**: Change multiple chapter prices at once
2. **Price Rules**: Min/max constraints, % change caps
3. **A/B Testing**: Test different prices for chapters
4. **Regional Pricing**: Different prices by region/customer
5. **Predictive Pricing**: ML-based optimal pricing
6. **Integration with Promotions**: Better promo/price coordination
